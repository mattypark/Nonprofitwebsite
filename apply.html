Three ways in.

There are three ways to join Axiom — as a member of a chapter, as a chapter lead in your city, or as part of the founding team. Each path runs the same program and feeds the same global pipeline.

— The three tracks

Pick the door that fits.

All three tracks ask the same thing first: write a short letter on what you actually want to build, and why. We read every one.

Track 01

Join a chapter

For high schoolers anywhere. Run through the AI, CS, and marketing tracks with a chapter near you. Most-passionate members earn internship placements at real startups.

Start application →
Track 02

Lead a chapter

For students who want to start the first chapter in their city. You get the curriculum, mentorship, and the Stanford KID rollout playbook — you bring the room and the energy.

Start application →
Track 03

Founding team

Two seats remain on the founding team. We're looking for the most passionate builders — people who want a real hand in shaping how Axiom grows worldwide.

Start application →
— The letter

One letter. That's the application.

No résumé. No GPA. No prior experience required. Just write a few paragraphs on what you want to build, why it matters to you, and which track you're applying to.

Q1

What do you want to build?

The thing — the actual thing — you'd want to spend a year working on. Be specific. Describe it like it already exists.

Q2

Why does it matter to you?

What makes this not just interesting but worth your weekends? We pick by passion, so this question carries more weight than any other.

Q3

Which track?

Member, chapter lead, or founding team. If you're not sure, pick the one that feels closest and tell us why.
