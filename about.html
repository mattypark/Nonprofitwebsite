Axiom/About

The founders, the mission, and the partner.

Axiom is a global student-led nonprofit teaching high schoolers the foundations of modern startups — AI, computer science, and marketing. From each chapter, the most passionate students go on to intern at real startups around the world.

Status501(c)(3) in formation BaseLouisville, KY · operating globally PartnerStanford KID
— The mission

Build the program we wished existed in high school.

High schoolers don't lack ambition — they lack a path. Axiom gives them one: chapters around the world that teach AI, CS, and marketing, and from each chapter, a way out into real startups.

01

Chapters everywhere

A local chapter in your city teaching the foundations of modern startups — AI, computer science, and marketing.

02

Selected by passion

The most passionate members from each chapter — not the most credentialed — earn a spot to intern at real startups, globally.

03

Real work, real stakes

The internships are real. The startups are real. The work ships. Nothing about Axiom is a simulation of the thing.

— The partner

Working with Stanford KID on the global rollout.

We're partnering with Stanford KID to plan how Axiom expands chapter by chapter, country by country — making sure each new chapter has the curriculum, mentorship, and pathway that the program is built on.
