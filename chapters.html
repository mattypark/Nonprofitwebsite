Axiom/Chapters

One program, everywhere.

A chapter is where Axiom actually happens. Each one runs the same curriculum — AI, computer science, marketing — and feeds the same pipeline of passionate students into real startup internships, anywhere on earth.

NetworkGlobal · 5 launching PartnerStanford KID StatusFounding chapters open · Fall '26
— How a chapter runs

A small team, a real curriculum, and a way out.

Chapters are student-led. Two student leads run the meeting cadence with mentor support. The curriculum and pathway come from Axiom. The energy comes from the room.

01

Weekly meetings

Every chapter runs weekly sessions across the three tracks — AI, CS, and marketing — plus shared time to build alongside each other.

02

Student-led, mentor-backed

Two student leads run the chapter, supported by mentors, alumni, and the central Axiom team. Stanford KID supports the global rollout.

03

A path into a real startup

The most passionate members from each chapter earn an internship at a real startup, globally. Selected for effort, not credentials.
