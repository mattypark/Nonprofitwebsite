Axiom/FAQ

The questions worth asking.

A short FAQ on chapters, eligibility, the selection process, and what an internship actually looks like. If yours isn't here, write to us — we'll add it.

Q · 01

Who can join Axiom?

Any high schooler, anywhere. There's no application bar based on grades, prior coding experience, or credentials. The only thing we look for is genuine interest in startups, AI, CS, or marketing — and a willingness to put in the time.

Q · 02

What does a chapter actually do?

Each chapter runs weekly sessions across three tracks — AI, computer science, and marketing — plus shared time to build alongside one another. Chapters are student-led with mentor support, and follow a curriculum the central Axiom team maintains.

Q · 03

How are members selected for internships?

By passion, not credentials. The most passionate members from each chapter — measured by the time and care they put into the program — earn placements at real startups. We've made this a deliberate choice: effort beats résumé every time.

Q · 04

What does an internship look like?

Real work at a real startup. Placements are global, so the company you intern at could be anywhere. We're being conservative about specifics until our legal framework around high-school internships is finalized — we'd rather promise less and deliver more.

Q · 05

How much time does it take?

Chapter members commit a few hours a week — one weekly session plus building time. Chapter leads commit more. The members who go on to internships are typically the ones who choose to put in extra time on their own.

Q · 06

Is there a cost to join?

No. Axiom is free for all members. We're a 501(c)(3) in formation — funding comes from sponsors, partners, and donors, not from students.

Q · 07

What is Stanford KID?

Stanford KID is a partner helping us plan how Axiom rolls out chapter by chapter, country by country. They're focused on the global expansion playbook — making sure each new chapter inherits a working program rather than starting from zero.

Q · 08

Can I start a chapter in my city?

Yes. If there isn't a chapter where you live, you can apply to start the first one. You get the curriculum, mentorship, and rollout support; you bring the room and the energy.

Q · 09

How do I apply?

Write a short letter — what you want to build, why it matters to you, and which track you're applying to (member, chapter lead, or founding team). That's it. No résumé, no GPA, no prior experience required.

Question not answered?
