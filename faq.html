Axiom/FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything we've been asked about cost, time commitment, eligibility, and how to join the founding team. Still missing something? Reach out.

Who can apply? +

Any high school student with a serious interest in AI or computer science. You don't need a perfect GPA or a long resume — we care most about curiosity, follow-through, and how you think.

Does it cost anything? +

No. Axiom is a nonprofit. Participation is free, and we're building it to stay that way.

How much time does a cohort take? +

Expect 5–8 hours per week for 8 weeks. That includes a weekly team sync, mentor office hours, and the research or building work itself.

Do I need prior AI/ML experience? +

No — but you should be comfortable reading code and willing to learn fast. Teams are matched so beginners and advanced students can both contribute meaningfully.

How do I join the founding team? +

We're actively recruiting a Tech Lead, Operations Lead, and Content / Publishing Lead. Reach out via the apply link and tell us what role you're most drawn to and why.
