# Axiom *(working name)*

A student-led nonprofit giving high schoolers the teams, mentorship, and platform to do real work at the intersection of AI research **and** startups — then turn it into something people actually use.

## The idea

Most high school research programs stop at the paper. Most startup / internship programs never touch research. Both sides leave a lot on the table: research rarely gets applied, and builders rarely go deep. Axiom connects the two.

We run the program as **two divisions that collaborate**, not as isolated tracks.

### 1. Research Division
Students work in teams of 3–5 on focused topics in AI, ML, and CS. The output isn't just a PDF — every research project is paired with a follow-through: a prototype, a tool, a demo, or a product built on top of the finding. If you publish a paper with Axiom, you ship *something* with it.

- Output: paper **+** an applied artifact (product, model, tool, or open-source repo).
- Published on the *Axiom Journal* — a mini arXiv for high school research, designed so the work is actually discoverable and useful.

### 2. Startup Division
Students get placed with partner startups to work on a specific project — as an intern, shadow, or embedded contributor. Real product work, real engineering, real problems. Think of it as a structured way for high schoolers to get their hands on the kind of work that usually requires being a college junior with a network.

- Output: shipped work inside a real startup (feature, tool, research spike, internal model, etc.).
- Mentorship from founders and engineers at the partner companies.

### Cross-division collaboration
The two divisions aren't walled off. Research teams surface findings that startup teams can apply to their partner company's problem. Startup teams surface real-world problems that research teams can dig into. The goal is a loop: research → application → new questions → more research. High schoolers get both the depth *and* the ship-it experience.

## Who we're looking for

- **Researchers** — high schoolers who want to dig into an AI/ML question, publish it, and then build something with what they learned.
- **Builders / startup track** — high schoolers who'd rather ship than speculate and want real embedded time inside a startup.
- **Partner startups** — early- to growth-stage companies willing to scope a real project for a high school contributor.
- **Founding team** — 3 board roles still open:
  - **Tech Lead** — manages projects and mentors teams across both divisions.
  - **Operations Lead** — organizes cohorts, partner-startup pipeline, scheduling, and logistics.
  - **Content / Publishing Lead** — runs the website and the Axiom Journal.
