# Axiom *(working name)*

A student-led nonprofit giving high schoolers the teams, mentorship, and platform to do real AI and computer science research — then publish it.

## What we do

Three core programs:

1. **Research Teams** — Students work in groups of 3–5 on focused topics in AI, ML, and data science. Output: a paper, blog, or report.
2. **Build Projects** — Teams ship real apps, tools, or AI models (e.g., an AI tutor, a productivity app, a model benchmark).
3. **Publishing Platform** — Finished work lives on the *Axiom Journal*, a mini arXiv for high school research.

## Who we're looking for

- **Researchers** — high schoolers who want to dig into an AI/ML question and publish a paper on it.
- **Builders** — high schoolers who'd rather ship than speculate and want to release real open-source projects.
- **Founding team** — 3 board roles still open:
  - **Tech Lead** — manages projects and mentors teams.
  - **Operations Lead** — organizes cohorts, scheduling, and logistics.
  - **Content / Publishing Lead** — runs the website and the journal.
