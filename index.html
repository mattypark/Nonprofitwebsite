05 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about Axiom, how cohorts work, and what we publish.

Who can apply to Axiom? +

Any high school student with a serious interest in AI or computer science. No perfect GPA required — we care about curiosity and follow-through.

How does Axiom's research model work? +

Cohorts of 3–5 students are placed on a specialized team with a mentor. Over 8 weeks, each team produces a paper, a shipped project, and a publication on the Axiom Journal.

Does it cost anything? +

No. Axiom is a nonprofit. Participation is free, and we intend to keep it that way.

How much time does a cohort take? +

Expect 5–8 hours per week over 8 weeks — weekly team syncs, mentor office hours, and the research or building work itself.

What kinds of projects do teams ship? +

Papers, open-source toolkits, AI apps, benchmark studies, and research demos. Every cohort publishes work on the Axiom Journal with a permanent URL.

Is Axiom suitable for beginners? +

Yes — teams are matched so beginners and advanced students can both contribute meaningfully. You should be comfortable reading code and willing to learn fast.
